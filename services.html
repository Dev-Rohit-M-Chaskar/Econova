Service 01

Residential Rooftop

Complete rooftop solar solutions for homes, from site assessment and system design to installation, net-metering support and commissioning. Econova manages the entire process to deliver a reliable, efficient and hassle-free solar solution tailored to each household’s energy needs.

Discuss Your Project

Suitable Applications

  • Independent houses and residential properties
  • Villas, bungalows and row houses
  • Residential apartment buildings and societies
  • Homes with suitable rooftop space for solar installation

Scope

  • Rooftop assessment and electricity consumption analysis
  • Solar system sizing and customized system design
  • Selection and procurement of solar panels, inverters and mounting structures
  • Installation, electrical connections and system testing
  • Net metering coordination and grid connection support
  • Commissioning, documentation and system handover

Benefits

  • Reduce monthly electricity expenses through solar power generation
  • Customized system based on household energy requirements and Better utilization of available rooftop space
  • Reliable and clean source of renewable energy
  • Professional installation with quality-focused execution
  • Monitoring and maintenance support for long-term performance
  • Government subsidy of up to ₹78,000 is available for eligible residential rooftop solar projects and for society is 18000 per KW up to 500 KW under the PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana.

Service 02

Industrial Rooftop Solar

Customized rooftop solar solutions for factories, manufacturing facilities and industrial buildings — engineered around real roof structure, load capacity, production patterns and electrical infrastructure.

Get a Site Assessment

Suitable Applications

  • Factories and manufacturing units
  • Processing plants with daytime loads
  • Industrial parks and estate units
  • Sheds, warehouses and covered work areas

Scope

  • Roof structural assessment and load verification
  • Shadow analysis and layout optimization
  • Design of mounting, electrical and protection systems
  • Installation with production-area safety protocols
  • Testing, net-metering coordination and handover

Benefits

  • Direct reduction in daytime electricity costs
  • Utilization of otherwise unused roof space
  • Systems matched to the factory's load profile
  • Engineering designed for the specific roof condition

Ground-Mounted Solar

Discuss Your Project
