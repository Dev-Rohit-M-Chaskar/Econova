Why Econova

A Solar EPC Partner Focused on Getting the Details Right

Solar projects are won or lost in the details — engineering assumptions, material quality, coordination and follow-through. Here is how we handle those details.

Engineering Before Installation

We treat the site survey as the start of the project, not a formality. Roof structure, load capacity, shadows, electrical infrastructure and consumption data are studied before design begins.

The result is a system designed for your specific roof and load — not a standard layout applied to every building. Engineering drawings, sizing calculations and project documents are prepared before any installation work starts.

Quality in Execution

Quality on a solar site is a series of small checks made at the right moments: materials verified on arrival, mounting structures inspected before panels go up, cabling and terminations checked before energization, and testing completed before grid connection.

These checks are built into the project schedule, documented, and reported — so quality is demonstrated in records, not just claimed in meetings.

Clear Project Coordination

Every project runs on a defined plan: milestones, deliverables, responsibilities and reporting intervals. You know what happens next, when it happens, and who is accountable for it.

Coordination with utilities, approvals and third-party checks is handled within the same structure, so nothing is left to happen "at the end" of the project.

Project-Specific Solutions

No two sites are identical. Roof material, orientation, load patterns, space constraints and electrical infrastructure all differ — and the design must reflect that.

We size and engineer each system around the site's actual conditions, and we are straightforward about what makes sense for your facility when it does not.

Support Beyond Commissioning

A solar plant's value is realized over decades, not on handover day. Our support continues after commissioning — through maintenance guidance, performance monitoring, and a dependable point of contact as your plant matures.

This is why our O&M service exists as a structured offering, not an afterthought.

Our Commitment

What You Can Expect When You Work With Us

  • Honest site findings — if a project does not make sense for your facility, we will tell you.
  • Defined scope, budgets and timelines before work begins.
  • Quality checks that are documented, not assumed.
  • Regular, straight-forward progress reporting.
  • Support that continues after the commissioning date.
