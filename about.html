About Econova

Building Practical Solar Solutions for a Changing Energy Landscape

Econova Power Solution works with commercial, industrial and institutional organizations across Maharashtra to plan, build and maintain reliable solar power systems.

Who We Are

A Solar EPC Company Built on Execution

Econova Power Solution is a solar EPC company based in Ahilyanagar, Maharashtra, serving the commercial, industrial and institutional segments. We handle the complete project journey — assessment, engineering, procurement, installation and commissioning — with a focus on practical, site-specific execution.

Our work is defined by structure: clear documentation, defined milestones, quality checks and transparent communication. Every project is treated as an engineering problem to be solved for a specific site, not a standard package to be repeated.

Our Approach

Engineering First, Every Time

We begin with the site, not the catalogue. Roof condition, structure, shadow patterns, load-bearing capacity, electrical infrastructure and consumption profiles are studied before any design is prepared.

The design stage converts those findings into a detailed project plan — system sizing, layout, electrical drawings, procurement schedule and execution timeline — so that installation proceeds with clarity and accountability.

Quality is guarded at each stage: material verification on arrival, inspections during installation, testing before grid connection and a structured handover on commissioning.
