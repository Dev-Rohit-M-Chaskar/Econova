Our Process

From Site Assessment to Solar Power

  1. 01

    Site Assessment

    On-site evaluation of roof, land, shadow and electrical conditions.

  2. 02

    Feasibility & Design

    Technical feasibility, yield estimates and system design studies.

  3. 03

    Engineering

    Structural, electrical and civil engineering for the specific site.

  4. 04

    Procurement

    Sourcing of quality equipment with clear specifications and timelines.

  5. 05

    Installation

    Systematic installation following engineering drawings and safety practices.

  6. 06

    Commissioning

    Testing, grid connection and handover with performance verification.
